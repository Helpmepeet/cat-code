1. Let $S$ be the set of all ordered pairs $(x, y)$ of nonnegative integers $0 \le x \le 19$ and $0 \le y \le 2$. Compute the number of permutations $(x_1, y_1)$, $(x_2, y_2)$, $\ldots$, $(x_{60}, y_{60})$ of the elements of $S$ such that
- $y_1 = 2$ and $y_{60} = 0$;
- for all nonnegative integers $1 \le i \le 59$, exactly one of the following holds:
  - $x_i = x_{i+1}$ and $|y_i - y_{i+1}| = 1$,
  - $y_i = y_{i+1}$ and $x_i - x_{i+1}$ is $-1$ or $19$.
