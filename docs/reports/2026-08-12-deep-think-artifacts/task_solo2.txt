1. The integers from $1$ to $64$ are placed in some order into an $8\times 8$ grid of cells with one number in each cell. Let $a_{i,j}$ be the number placed in the cell in row $i$ and column $j$, and let $M$ be the sum of the absolute differences between adjacent cells. That is, \[M=\sum^{8}_{i=1}\sum^{7}_{j=1}\left(|a_{i,j+1}-a_{i,j}|+|a_{j+1,i}-a_{j,i}|\right).\] Find the remainder when the maximum possible value of $M$ is divided by $1000$.
